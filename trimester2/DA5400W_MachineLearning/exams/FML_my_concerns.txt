Q6 I selected "R^2 cannot be negative" but that's wrong answer because 
while for OLS linear regression with intercept, R^2 (on training data) is between 0-1 and equal to square of correlation coefficient r^2, it's NOT in general actually a "square":
* (evaluation metric, generally measured on test data NOT training data) R^2 = 1 - SSE (sum of squared errors) / TSS (total sum of squares)
* if we foce no intercept, even training R^2 can be negative
* R^2 = 0 means model as good as just predicting mean value every time. >0 means better, <0 means worse than predicting mean value every time 
  (latter is bad, can happen if test data is out of distribution)
