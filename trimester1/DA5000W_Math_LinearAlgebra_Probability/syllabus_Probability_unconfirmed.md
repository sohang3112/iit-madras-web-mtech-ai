MIDSEM PROBABILITY SYLLABUS (ie things in lectures so far):

*Lecture 1 & 2 PDF*
- random experiment, random variable (RV), sample space
    - effect of adding / multiplying constant to random variable on Expected Value, Variance, Standard Deviation
- continous (Probability Mass Function (PMF)), discrete (Probability Density Function (PDF)), Cumulative Distributive Function (CDF) for both discrete, continous
    - formulae for discrete, continous cases: total probability sum = 1, (expected value, variance) formulae
    - (contionous PDF) probablity of only range is defined, single event probability is 0
- central tendency: mean, median, mode
- 4 Moments: mean, median, **skew, kurtosis** (formulae for skew and kurtosis not covered, only concept)
- Mean Absolute Deviation (MAD), variance, standard deviation 
    - *Bessel's Correction* only when sampling: use N-1 instead of N in denominator in variance and standard deviation formulae
- Inter-Quartile Range: min, 25% Q1, median (50% Q2), 75% Q3, max
- Joint & Marginal probability
- Conditional Probability, Bayes Theorem (including heirachical bayes)
- Value of Information: EVPI, EVSI

*Lecture 3 PDF*
- Plots/graphs: histogram / bar graph, scatter plot, heatmap, box plot, pie chart (rarely used in ML)
- Distributions (PDF, CDF, Mean, Variance of each):
    - Bernoulli (discrete - 2 outputs: success / failure)
    - Uniform (discrete or continous)
    - Binomial & Negative Binomial (discrete)
    - Poisson (discrete)
    - Normal / Gaussian (continous), Normal approximation of Binomial and Poisson
    - Exponential (continous): *only distribution specifically measuring time*
    - Geometric & Hyper-Geometric (discrete)
