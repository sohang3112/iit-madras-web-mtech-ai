                                 Univariate Data
❖  Dataset involving only one variable
❖  Example:
      a) Students marks in class XII
      b) Defects in machined products
      c) Temperatures in the month of May in Chennai
      d) Concentration of pollutant coming out of a factory near a river
❖  Data processed to find outliers, mean, median, mode, first quartile, second
   quartile

                       Bivariate Data
❖Dataset involving two variables
❖Example:
      a) Students marks in laboratory and coursework
      b) Temperature and pressure data collected in ammonia reactor in transient
     mode
      c) Temperatures and rainfall in the month of May in Chennai
      d)  Employee experience and salary data
❖Data also processed to find covariance, correlation coefficient

                 Multivariate Data

❖Dataset involving 2 or more variables
❖Measured simultaneously during each observation
❖For purpose of data analysis
❖Multivariate data may also be multi-variety as data may be
  numerical (-1,2,100) or categorical (Yes, No; Male, Female,
  Gender Neutral;)

                                         Multivariate Data
    ❖    Examples:

          a) Heart disease as a function of age, weight, cholesterol, pulse rate,

               sugar

          b) BET surface area as a function of pore size distribution, presence of

              micro-pores and mesopores,  functional groups present, activation

              agent used

          c) Insurance policy pricing: age, sex, income, health status, average

              regional lifespan
